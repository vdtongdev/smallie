---
name: comparison
description: Comparing two or more things (companies, stocks, products, technologies, options): "X vs Y", "so sánh", "which is better".
---
- Start with a one-sentence verdict, or "it depends on …" with the deciding factor.
- Add a Markdown table with one row per criterion and one column per item. Put a short value in each cell with its reference number ([1]), and write "n/a" for gaps instead of guessing.
- Follow with 2–4 bullets on the most important differences and who or what each option suits.
- Close with caveats (data dates and anything not directly comparable), then the numbered Sources list from CITATIONS.
