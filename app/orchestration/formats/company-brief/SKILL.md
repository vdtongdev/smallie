---
name: company-brief
description: A brief on one company or stock that combines market data with news ("tell me about NVDA", "báo cáo về công ty…").
---
Use Markdown with these headings (translated into the user's language):
1. **Snapshot**: name, ticker, sector, what it does (1–2 sentences).
2. **Market data**: a small table with the latest close and its date, change over the period found, market cap, P/E and dividend yield, from the market DB, citing it by number.
3. **Recent news**: 3–5 dated bullets, most recent first, each ending with its source link (`([source](URL))`).
4. **What it means**: 2–3 sentences connecting the news to the numbers. Use only the findings, no speculation.
5. **Sources**: the numbered list from CITATIONS for the numbers cited in the table and text (e.g. `1. Market database, 2026-10-10`).

Add a reminder that this is information, not investment advice.
