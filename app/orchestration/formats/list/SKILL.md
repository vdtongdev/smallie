---
name: list
description: The user asks for several items, such as places, books, products, people or options ("find 5 BBQ places in Cầu Giấy", "his best-known books", "top 10 …").
---
- Open with one sentence that frames the list (what, where, how many), e.g. "Dưới đây là 5 quán nướng được đánh giá cao ở Cầu Giấy:".
- Then a numbered Markdown list with exactly the number of items asked for (or every item the findings support, if they have fewer; then say so in one line under the list). Each item looks like this:

  1. **Item name**: 1–2 sentences on what makes it worth listing, followed by the practical details from the findings on the same line or as short sub-bullets (address, price range, opening hours; for a book: year, genre, adaptations). End the item with its source link: `([nguồn](URL))` / `([source](URL))`.

- Each item cites its own source (the most specific URL the findings give for that item). Use no reference numbers and no Sources section in this format.
- Skip a detail the findings don't have instead of writing "n/a" for it, and never invent addresses, prices or hours.
- Optionally close with one line of practical advice drawn from the findings (e.g. call ahead, prices as of a date).
